How the implied value is worked out

One function prices every row, from the snapshot's own figures only. Start with the protocol's TVL. Multiply by the peer multiple: the winsorized median market-cap/TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (when a category has fewer than four live peers, the median of live protocols sharing its chains; failing that, the global median). Then adjust: growth — half of the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%, because one week is a signal, not a destiny; maturity — 0.60 for a brand-new listing rising to 1.00 after 1,095 days tracked with no token; breadth — +3% per extra chain, capped at +15%. A soft sanity cap holds a value to twice the largest live peer's market cap in its category, with only 5% of any excess past the cap counting, so the biggest protocols keep their size order instead of tying.
